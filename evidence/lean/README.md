# Conditional historical Lean fragments

These files are retained as historical mathematical context, not as a complete Lean submission. The finite certificate hypotheses and analytic inequalities are not discharged by merely possessing these reductions.

This directory intentionally does not claim a complete standalone build, a new successful build, or an unconditional formal proof of the all-n theorem. The mathematical submission relies on the paper and its checkable certificates, not on Lean credit.

`FidelityMin.lean` contains copied statement text from formal-conjectures. Its upstream notice and the applicable license are preserved; see `../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
