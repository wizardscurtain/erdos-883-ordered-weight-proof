## Proposed contribution

The proposed new result is an alternative computer-assisted argument for the all-n odd-cycle assertion in Erdős Problem 883(i). The methodological contribution concerns the adjusted-weight pair estimate, ordered interior skeleton, complementary connector criteria, and their finite-to-infinite certificate assembly.

The exact theorem and its supporting evidence must be assessed together. Public repository ownership and an author line identify the submitted work; they do not independently certify correctness or establish historical priority.
